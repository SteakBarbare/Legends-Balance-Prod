# Legends Balance Prod

This is a mod for Gladius Relic of War made to balance the Legends mods without having to constantly harass the mod creator, Devian.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> Some changes are supposed to be definitive, while others are here purely to quickly patch something in the dirtiest way possible since there are some limitations and / or I'm stoopid.
> I mostly play PVE with friends in impossible difficulty (2V2V2V2V2 or 3V3V3V3), so the changes were made with this in mind
> The main thing I want to avoid is having almost unkillable units since the AI doesn't know how to handle them / allowing to player to have an exponential economy way too early in the game
> All these changes are made to be optimal with my mod [collection](https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3039477074&savesuccess=1)

## Changes
## Factions

### Spess Mehreens
> [!NOTE]
> The main problem with the Spess Mehreens are their heroes, a lot of them can become raid bosses in no time and apply stacking buffs / auras in a 3 tiles radius
> Also the infantry is supposed to be elite and tanky, but some were way too overpowered (e.g: Medusan Immortals)
- Reduced Ranged damage res for Medusan Immortals from 66% to 33%, they could tank even late game unit before
- Reduced their weapon range 2 -> 1
- Gordian heal aura range 3 -> 1, SM already have some of the best infantry in the game, and having a hero that can heal almost all your army by just exisiting was way too strong, now you will need to use ur brain to fink about positioning
- Most building will now give requisition to the AI, it's not the prettiest fix but SM AI was struggling because it was spending all its money droping buildings
- Nothing to do with HoL, but I buffed the Whirlwhind since it was really weak af

#### Kay-Ohss
> [!NOTE]
> The best faction with the mods imo
> I mainly nerfed Nurgle units since their passive was making them unkillable if inside a poison area (and I'm a DG fan :c)
> Also Abbadon is supposed to be good, but he had some crazy buff that could transform any unit into a destroyer of worlds
- Death Guard passive gives way less invulnerable damage reduction
- Thagus food prod on kill went from 2 -> 0.2, he could take care of your entire food production with his passive
- Sublime passive (can't remember who has it, maybe the hero scorpion ?) has reduced ranged damage reduction
- Warmaster damage reduction buff from a lot to 5%

#### T'au
> [!NOTE]
> I should rework their whole economy since they just get ridiculous loyalty bonuses, but I'm way too lazy...
> Also maybe I'll tweak some units in the future, like the swordships or some late game stuff
- Conviction from the Gue'Vesa hero gives less influence
- Greater good from blue ethereal named hero (his real name is too hard to rememeber) gives less FNP

#### Sisters of Battle
> [!NOTE]
> Sarane, Sarane and Sarane, she can carry a game alone, dead or alive
- Sarane Reliquaries buff doesn't give damage reduction anymore, the damages and movement buff are already OP as it is and the buffed unit at least need to ba able to die
- Sarane buff on death damage and morale loss were reduced to 10%, it was making killing her a way bigger problem than letting her alive, most of the time we were just ignoring her

#### Toasters
> [!NOTE]
> There is a lot to do with them, their vanilla late game is already extremely powerful, but here with the new units it gets to another level
> Maybe I'll do more in the future, but I'm still too lazy for now to try to balance them properly
- Dahan permanent buff where too strong for their price so I nerfed the unkeep for all units type
- Dahan himself was too strong so I got rid of some of his inv saves, now he's still a powerhouse, but at least late game units can get rid of this early / mid game hero without spending 10 turns attacking him (and I'm not event talking about his passive when he's low life with the damage he could take before going down)
- Loyalty from Code Chivalric (Leviathan hero) 1 -> 0.1 per kill, with his multiple weapons he could give you a stacking 3+ loyalty on each of your city each turn, I don't think I need to explain why this is busted

#### Tyranids
> [!NOTE]
> Nah, nope, nuhuh, I won't even touch them, they are so unstoppable that I don't even know where to begin with...

#### Necrons
> [!NOTE]
> Lotsa new units, but I haven't checked what's really OP or not
- Trazyn mind control range 3 -> 1
- More cooldown on his resurection, now you actually risk something when trying to mind control / capture a pokemon

#### Orks
> [!NOTE]
> Orks were fine but there is a lot of stuff being added, and most importantly is the buildings
> Now Orks have one of the best economy in the game since they can turn any ruin into anything they need, including loyalty
- Gretchins construction on ruins cost x6, this should be enough to prevent players from spamming them till the mid / late game without removing this cool mechanic
- To balance this Orks now have a second scrap drop

## Items

> [!NOTE]
> Most changes are for the stacking items (like each kill grants additional attack)
> These would make any hero a demigod just by killing some neutral units
- All stacking items have their bonus /10, so you have to stack longer and get more debuff before being OP, also this will prevent the auto equip on late game heroes since their upkeep will increase a lot
- Some items like the helmet have reduced FNP / Inv saves, these were way too common for the raw suvivability they offered to any hero wearing them
